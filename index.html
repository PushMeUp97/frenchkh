French A1 Level: The Fundamentals

1. What is CEFR A1 French?

The A1 Level represents the "Discovery" or beginner stage of the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR). At this level, your goal is to interact in a simple way with the world around you.

Core Competencies:

  • Introduce yourself and others simply.
  • Ask and answer basic personal questions (where you live, people you know, things you have).
  • Understand and use familiar everyday expressions and basic phrases aimed at the satisfaction of practical needs.
  • Communicate slowly if the other person talks clearly.

2. Key Grammar Pillars

A. Subject Pronouns (Les Pronoms Sujets)

These form the absolute foundation of building any French sentence.

Singular Plural
Je (I) Nous (We)
Tu (You - informal/singular) Vous (You - formal or plural)
Il / Elle / On (He / She / One, We) Ils / Elles (They - masc. / They - fem.)

B. Nouns and Articles (Gender & Number)

Every single noun in French has a grammatical gender: Masculine or Feminine.

  • Definite Articles (The): Le (masc.), La (fem.), L' (before a vowel), Les (plural).
  • Indefinite Articles (A/An/Some): Un (masc.), Une (fem.), Des (plural).
Example: Le livre (The book - masc.) | La table (The table - fem.) | Des pommes (Some apples - plural)

C. The Two Ultimate Essential Verbs

You cannot survive A1 French without mastering the irregular verbs Être (to be) and Avoir (to have).
